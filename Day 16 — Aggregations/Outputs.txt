[info] 26. AGGREGATION CONCEPTS
[info] ------------------------------------------------------------
[info] COUNT
[info] -----
[info] Counts rows.
[info] SUM
[info] ---
[info] Adds numeric values.
[info] AVG
[info] ---
[info] Calculates average.
[info] MIN
[info] ---
[info] Finds smallest value.
[info] MAX
[info] ---
[info] Finds largest value.
[info] GROUP BY
[info] --------
[info] Groups rows before aggregation.
[info] HAVING
[info] ------
[info] Filters groups after aggregation.
[info] DataFrame equivalent:
[info] groupBy()
[info]     |
[info]     v
[info] agg()
[info]     |
[info]     v
[info] filter()
[info] SQL equivalent:
[info] GROUP BY
[info]     |
[info]     v
