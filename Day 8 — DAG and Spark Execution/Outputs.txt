[info] 16. JOBS, STAGES, TASKS AND PARTITIONS
[info] ------------------------------------------------------------
[info] JOB:
[info] A job is created when an action is called.
[info] STAGE:
[info] A job is divided into stages around shuffle boundaries.
[info] TASK:
[info] A task is the unit of work executed for a partition.
[info] PARTITION:
[info] A partition is a chunk of an RDD.
[info] Relationship:
[info] Action -> Job -> Stages -> Tasks -> Partitions
